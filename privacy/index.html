Privacy

Small site, small footprint.

Arv.in and its project pages use limited, cookieless audience measurement to understand which pages are useful and how well they perform.

What is measured

Cloudflare Web Analytics measures visits, page views, referring pages, country, broad browser and device details, page-load times, and Core Web Vitals. These statistics help improve the site.

Analytics does not set cookies or use local storage, session storage, or other persistent browser identifiers. It does not track you across sites, create visitor profiles, record sessions, or collect what you type into forms. We do not send custom link-click or file-download events.

Cloudflare receives page and referrer information and browser performance measurements. Its Web Analytics service does not log URL query strings. Referring page paths may be included when your browser provides them. Cloudflare documents the data its beacon collects in its measurement reference.

Your browser controls

Analytics is not loaded when your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal. These controls can be enabled in supported browser or privacy-extension settings.

The site also works with JavaScript or analytics requests blocked. Local previews are never measured.

Appearance preference

If you choose light or dark mode, the site saves that choice in your browser’s local storage. It is used only to remember your appearance preference and is not sent to analytics. Until you choose, the site follows your operating system’s appearance.

Other services

This notice describes website analytics. Project apps, optional signup forms, downloads, and external websites may have their own privacy practices. Information you choose to submit to a signup form is separate from analytics.
