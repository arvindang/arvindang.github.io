Privacy

Small site, small footprint.

Arv.in uses limited, cookieless audience measurement to understand which pages are useful and whether the site performs well.

What is measured

PostHog records anonymous page views, page duration, broad browser and device details, referral origins, and outbound link or file-download destinations.

Analytics does not write cookies or local-storage identifiers, create person profiles, record sessions, capture form input, or store IP addresses. URL query strings and fragments are removed before events are sent, and referrers are reduced to their origin.

Your browser controls

Analytics is not loaded when your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal. These controls can be enabled in supported browser or privacy-extension settings.
