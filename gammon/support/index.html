GAMMON / SUPPORT

We’re here to help.

Questions about a match, a lesson, or syncing your progress? Send us a note and tell us which device you’re using.

CONTACT GAMMON SUPPORT

support@arvindang.com ↗

For a bug report, include your device, OS version, the app version from Settings, and the steps that led to the problem. Please do not send private match details unless they help explain the issue.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Matches, lessons, and the coach run on your device. iCloud sync needs a connection if you choose to use it.

Where are my matches saved?

Gammon saves matches and progress on your device. When iCloud sync is on and available, it also keeps a copy in your private iCloud storage for your other devices. You can switch sync off in Gammon Settings.

Do I need an account?

No Gammon account is needed. iCloud sync uses the Apple account already signed in on your device.

How do I find the version?

Open Gammon Settings and scroll to the bottom. Include the displayed version and build number when contacting support.

For details about saved data, sync, and deletion, read the Gammon privacy policy.
